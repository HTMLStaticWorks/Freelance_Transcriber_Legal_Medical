About MedLex

Bridging the gap between spoken word and precise unalterable text through expert transcription services.

Our Story

Precision You Can Rely On

Founded in 2022, MedLex emerged from a critical need for highly accurate, sector-specific transcription. General transcribers often missed nuanced legal terminology and complex medical jargon, leading to costly errors.

Our founders, a unique partnership between a seasoned paralegal and a former healthcare administrator, built a specialized team trained rigorously in HIPAA protocols, advanced vocabulary, and the structural formatting required by courts and hospitals.
