Service Overview

Legal proceedings demand perfect accuracy. A single mistranscribed word can alter the outcome of a case. We provide certified legal transcription services for law firms, legal departments, and court reporting agencies.

Supported Formats

  • Depositions and witness testimonies
  • Court hearings and arbitrations
  • Interrogations and police reports
  • Client correspondence and legal briefs

Security Measures

All documents are processed strictly within a closed, SOC 2 compliant environment. Files are deleted from our servers voluntarily after 30 days of completion unless archived by request.
